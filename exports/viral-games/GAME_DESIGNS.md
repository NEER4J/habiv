# 50 unique game designs

Every game gets its own mechanic, feedback loop, scene language, and title-screen treatment. The upload ZIPs are rebuilt from these designs; thumbnails remain paired by slug.

1. **Blink Buffet** — Hunt the one green bite among decoys; wrong taps break the streak. Kiosk-style service menu with a neon kitchen scene.
2. **Jelly Stack** — Place a falling jelly on a wobbling tower and land it close to the center; three misses collapse the stack. Soft recipe-card menu with a dessert counter scene.
3. **Midnight Checkout** — Inspect four checkout panels and pick the one reflection that is wrong. Redacted security-terminal menu with a fluorescent store scene.
4. **Pocket Planet** — Rotate a miniature planet to catch climate seeds while avoiding cold shadows. Circular planetarium menu with orbital stars.
5. **Rooftop Relay** — Switch between three rooftops just before the runner reaches a gap. Split-screen race menu with a rain-soaked skyline.
6. **Two-Tap Tango** — Tap on the pulse, alternating between two lanes; early or late taps lose the combo. Dance-floor menu with beat rings and laser lighting.
7. **Moss & Mugs** — Serve the drink that matches each customer’s order before the café queue fills. Handwritten café menu with a warm counter scene.
8. **Emoji Alibi** — Choose the one emoji that contradicts a short visual story. Chat-card menu with an evidence-board puzzle scene.
9. **Pigeon Panic** — Move the cart and catch falling chips while avoiding the pigeon shadow. Street-food menu with a bright city-stall scene.
10. **Cloud Save** — Steer a paper boat into rain, sun, or thunder targets to complete weather recipes. Postcard menu with a layered sky scene.
11. **Orbit Snack** — Time a launch so the snack enters the moon’s green gravity window. Planetary launch menu with a dark space scene.
12. **Color Debt** — Pay the highlighted color debt by tapping matching tiles before the balance locks. Ledger menu with a geometric tile board.
13. **One More Tile** — Rotate path tiles to connect a spark to an exit before the sliding tile moves again. Blueprint menu with a technical line-art scene.
14. **Cat Nap Courier** — Route parcels to the correct sleeping cat while avoiding the noise zones. Apartment noticeboard menu with a moonlit hallway.
15. **Beat the Buzzer** — Hit the buzzer only when the ring is in the safe color band. Game-show menu with a spotlight and countdown dial.
16. **Doomscroll Dungeon** — Swipe through three cursed posts and choose the one that continues the map. Vertical-feed menu with a phone-shaped dungeon scene.
17. **Tiny Train Jam** — Switch the train to the track that has the next snack crate. Ticket-window menu with a miniature valley railway.
18. **Scream Test 1999** — Repeat a diagnostic sequence of four monitor signals before the waveform turns hostile. CRT boot menu with scanlines and tape noise.
19. **Pondbound** — Hop to the next safe lily pad while ripples close behind the frog. Nature-journal menu with a twilight pond.
20. **Lucky Lanterns** — Sort lanterns into the river order requested by the village. Folded-paper menu with a lantern-lit river.
21. **Beat Bloom** — Hit the lane carrying the next beat to grow a flower chain. Album-sleeve menu with a glowing music garden.
22. **Traffic Ghost** — Change lanes to overtake your previous ghost without touching traffic. Dashboard menu with a night freeway and ghost trails.
23. **Shelf Life** — Move each product into its matching shelf category before the shop closes. Inventory-sticker menu with a warm shop interior.
24. **Don’t Look Back** — Choose safe footsteps from four audio-visual cues without selecting the one behind you. Narrow hallway menu with a single exit light.
25. **Hot Potato Protocol** — Pass the potato to the bot holding the current safe color. Party-round menu with radial player positions.
26. **Snack Attack** — Move a lunchbox under falling food and catch only the requested snack. Lunch-tray menu with a conveyor-belt kitchen.
27. **Orbiting Room** — Drag furniture into the sunlight patch as the room rotates around a planet. Interior-design menu with a windowed orbital room.
28. **Plant Parent** — Match a plant’s visible symptom to the correct care action. Text-message menu with a botanical windowsill.
29. **Loop Detective** — Scrub four security frames and tap the single frame that changed. Evidence-folder menu with a repeating hallway monitor.
30. **Quickdraw Postcard** — Trace a shape between two postcard stamps before the ink dries. Postcard menu with paper, ink, and a traveling stamp.
31. **Moonbase Merge** — Merge equal machine tiles on a 4×4 moonbase grid to build the breakfast module. Mission-control menu with modular machinery.
32. **Late Night Laundromat** — Sort socks into the correct machine while keeping the cycle timer moving. Laundromat ticket menu with spinning drums and fluorescent light.
33. **Neon Noodle** — Drag a noodle through the open kitchen lane and collect broth bubbles. Takeout menu with a kinetic neon kitchen.
34. **Soundcheck 404** — Set a signal slider inside the clean band while static pushes it away. Broadcast-console menu with waveform scenery.
35. **Paper Plane Panic** — Tap gust lanes to keep the plane above desks and below ceiling fans. Folded-paper menu with a wind-tunnel office scene.
36. **Graveyard Shift** — Serve the correct graveyard customer based on their candle color and arrival order. Cemetery ledger menu with a moonlit shop window.
37. **Marble Runaway** — Tilt the track toward the open ramp while the marble keeps rolling. Toy-box menu with a close-up kinetic marble course.
38. **Signal / Noise** — Drag a filter knob until the hidden signal becomes clear. Audio-lab menu with an analog oscilloscope scene.
39. **Bubble Wrap Boss** — Pop the active bubble in each pattern row and avoid the decoy. Industrial punch-card menu with tactile bubble material.
40. **Daily Doodle Duel** — Connect three prompt points in the right order before the judge stamps the page. Artist-sketchbook menu with bold marker strokes.
41. **Tidepool Tactics** — Choose the tide gate that sends water to the stranded creature without flooding the shell. Coastal field-guide menu with an overhead tidepool.
42. **Night Market Dash** — Select the open market lane as stalls slide into the road. Vendor-sign menu with lanterns, steam, and scooter lights.
43. **Soft Reset** — Archive the noisy window and keep only the calm task in the desktop focus zone. Desktop boot menu with a soft morning workspace.
44. **Mango Meteor** — Slice ripe fruit while the meteor warning crosses the kitchen window. Recipe-poster menu with tropical color and flying juice.
45. **Houseplant Hotline** — Answer the plant call whose symptom matches the care card. Switchboard menu with cords, leaves, and afternoon sun.
46. **Four Beat Forecast** — Play the weather lane that completes the four-beat forecast. Weather-broadcast menu with a storm-to-sunrise panorama.
47. **Ghost in the Queue** — Call tickets in order while one invisible ticket moves between positions. Station-board menu with velvet ropes and a ghost ticket.
48. **Mirror Match** — Tap the tile that restores left/right symmetry before the mirror shifts again. Museum-label menu with a reflective geometric room.
49. **Kite String Theory** — Drag a kite through warm currents and away from storm pockets. Wind-map menu with a wide afternoon sky.
50. **Lucky Number 13** — Draw cards until the running sum enters the lucky range without hitting the reversal card. Velvet-card menu with a ritual table and glowing deck.
